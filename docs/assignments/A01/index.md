## Product Analyze

The product I selected is a vitamin D3 + K2 supplement container. Its primary mechanical function is to store and protect tablets while allowing the container to be repeatedly opened and released. The threaded cap converts rotational motion from the users had into axial motion and clamping force. 

<img width="204" height="229" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-24 at 11 42 56 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/4fe1b3a9-3330-49bf-875d-9e18e4fcad8f" />

The governing model is a sure thread relationship. the cap consists of an axial movement. This model assumes the cap and bottle threads are properly aligned and no not significantly deform during normal use. 

The bottle body has a cylindrical shape that efficiently stores the tables. Its narrow neck controls the opening and contains the external threads.

<img width="151" height="154" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-24 at 11 46 55 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/780b3938-3dad-49d7-a7cc-7cd17c484aca" />

The cap contains internal helical threads that engage the bottle threads. Its diameter provides leverage for tightening and loosening, while the rigid outer surface improves grip. 

<img width="193" height="145" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-24 at 11 47 57 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b23622f6-72c9-43e4-b328-4c0be3818ece" />


A representative patent is CA2281705A1, "Plastic Screw Top," invented by Gunter Krautkramer. The design uses internal cap threads that engage external threads on a bottle neck and includes sealing features to improve closure performance. 

Two alternatives are a snap fit lid, which uses elastic deformation to lock onto the container, and a flip top cap, which uses a hinge and latch instead of repeatedly removing the entire cap. 

One important design decision is the use of a threaded cap. Threads allow the user to create a controlled clamping force and repeatedly open and reseal the bottle. This makes the design reliable, simple e, and inexpensive to manufacture. 

Although the supplement bottle is simple, its geometry performs an important mechanical function. The bottle stores the tablets, while the threaded cap converts rotational motion into axial force to create a secure reusable closure. 

## Part 2: Decide

### 1. Homepage Identity

I designed my homepage so that a visitor can immediately understand that this portfolio contains my mechanical engineering coursework, projects, and technical documentation. The homepage gives a short explanation of the portfolio and uses clear navigation so the reader can quickly find each assignment. I chose to keep the homepage simple and organized because an engineering portfolio should allow the reader to find information quickly without unnecessary distractions.

### 2. One Intentional Customization

I changed the website color scheme to use darker blue and neutral colors. This change improves readability and gives the portfolio a more professional appearance that is appropriate for engineering documentation. The default color scheme was more visually generic and did not separate headings, navigation, and technical content as clearly.

### 3. Documentation Standard

For every assignment, I will clearly document the objective, process, important engineering decisions, results, and lessons learned with enough technical detail for another engineering student to understand my work.
